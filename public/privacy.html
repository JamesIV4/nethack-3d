NetHack 3D Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

NetHack 3D is developed and maintained by James Pound (“we,” “us”). This policy applies to NetHack 3D on Meta Quest, desktop, Android, and the web, and to this project website. The project is maintained at github.com/JamesIV4/nethack-3d.

1. Overview

NetHack 3D is a single-player game. You do not need a separate NetHack 3D account. Game progress and customization are stored on your device or in your browser. We do not operate a server that receives your saves, character names, or VR movement data, and we do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

The standalone Quest game can be played offline. When connected, update checks, hosting services, platform services, or links you choose to open can involve network requests as described below.

2. Data processed and why

  • Game data: character names you choose, character settings, saved games, scores, run history, and game messages are stored locally to run the game, resume adventures, and display your results. A character name does not need to be your real name.
  • Preferences and imported content: display, audio, language, controller, hotbar, and VR settings, along with tilesets and sound files you import, are stored locally to provide your chosen experience.
  • VR and control inputs: the app processes headset and controller poses, button and joystick input, and supported tracking information supplied by the headset runtime to render the scene and interpret your actions. This is processed on the device for gameplay, rather than uploaded to us as movement recordings. The Quest release does not request eye-tracking, microphone-recording, or location permissions.
  • Diagnostics: local logs can contain timestamps, app/build and browser/device information, errors, game messages, and input or interaction details. These help diagnose problems. The embedded runtime can also maintain local technical diagnostics. NetHack 3D does not automatically send these logs to the developer; if you choose to share them for support, they may include character names or other text you entered.
  • Support requests: if you submit a GitHub issue, we can see your GitHub username, messages, and attachments. We use them to respond and troubleshoot. Issues are public; do not include passwords, payment information, or other private data.

3. Network requests and third parties

GitHub: the app checks GitHub release information when startup update notifications are enabled, and when you manually check for updates. Supported desktop and Android clients may also download updates. You can disable startup version notifications in the app’s options. Playing the web version, viewing this policy, downloading releases, or following project links also contacts GitHub and its delivery services. These services receive ordinary request information such as your IP address, request time, requested resource, and browser/client information. The update check does not upload your saved games or VR poses. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Meta and your device platform: Meta independently processes information associated with your Meta account, store transactions, app distribution, headset services, and platform diagnostics under its own policies. NetHack 3D does not receive your payment-card details. See the Meta Privacy Policy. Other operating systems and browsers may provide their own diagnostics, storage, or backup services.

Local Quest communication: the bundled game uses an HTTP server at 127.0.0.1 on your headset. This address refers to the device itself, not an external game server.

Information you share: GitHub hosts public support issues under its own policy. Exported files and links opened outside the game are handled by the destination applications or services you choose.

4. Sharing and retention

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We use support information only to address your request and related maintenance or security needs. Public issues are hosted by GitHub. Information may also be disclosed when legally required.

Local game data remains until you delete it, clear the app/browser storage, uninstall the app, or your device/browser removes it. Diagnostic histories are bounded and older entries are replaced as new entries are recorded. Files you export remain wherever you save them until you delete them.

We keep correspondence and attachments for as long as needed to address the request and related support needs, unless you request deletion sooner. Limited records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or resolve a dispute. Third-party services have their own retention policies.

5. Deleting your data

These options are available to all users, regardless of location, without a deletion fee:

  • Individual saves and diagnostics: use the saved-game deletion controls or the Clear Logs control in the diagnostic-log dialog, where available.
  • All local data: clear NetHack 3D’s application storage using your device’s app-management controls, or uninstall the Quest/Android app. For the web version, clear site data for the NetHack 3D website in your browser. Desktop users can contact us for help locating the app’s local data. Deleting this data removes local progress and settings.
  • Exports and backups: delete any exported saves, logs, or other copies separately. Where your platform maintains backups, manage those through its backup controls.
  • Information shared in support issues: edit or delete your own GitHub comments using GitHub’s controls. To request removal of support information under our control, use the project’s issue tracker, identify the issue or comment, and request deletion without repeating personal details. For sensitive information posted publicly, you can also contact GitHub Support.

We will delete personal information under our control that is associated with your request, unless retention is legally required. If we cannot fulfill part of a request, we will explain why. We cannot remotely erase files held only on your device or independently erase records controlled by Meta or GitHub. No request to the developer is needed to delete your local game data.

6. Policy updates and contact

We may update this policy as the app or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current policy; prior versions are retained in the project’s Git history.
