DuskHack for NetHack 3.6

Credits
-------
Completed by clownmilk23, based on the first approximately 200 tiles of
DragonDePlatino's Unnamed NetHack Tileset. The completed DuskHack release
was posted by clownmilk23 on July 15, 2024. Its author confirmed compatibility
with NetHack 3.6.7 in the release discussion.
