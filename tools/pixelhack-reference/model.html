02 / VISUAL LANGUAGE

Braced for an attack.

A raised head, spread jaws, and lifted forelegs give the ant an aggressive silhouette. Smooth carapace, limbs, jaws, and antennae meet at distinct dark joints.

03 / MOTION

Idle holds the threatening pose with tense forelegs, working jaws, and probing antennae. Walk lowers the front legs into an alternating tripod gait. Attack launches forward and upward toward a taller target, snaps the jaws, and lands back in idle.

Walk plays in place. A game controller supplies travel. This study has not been integrated into gameplay.
